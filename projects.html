Project Mirari

🌱 Founder · Financial literacy initiative

A student-led initiative empowering underprivileged communities through accessible financial education. I led on-the-ground research in Sectors 16 & 17, Rohini, and ran workshops on saving, UPI, PMJDY and Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana.

  • Designed a micro-savings circle mobilising 50+ youth into self-managed groups that pool funds and track interest.
  • Expanded with Sensitivity Workshops promoting empathy, dignity and inclusion.
