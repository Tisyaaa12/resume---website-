About me

I'm a student pursuing a BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore alongside a BSc in Money, Banking & Finance at the University of Birmingham Dubai. I love understanding how markets move, and how money can be used to open doors for people.

From equity research internships to teaching financial literacy in my own community, I like work where numbers meet people. I've also spent years in Model UN and pitching competitions, so I'm comfortable researching, debating and leading a team.

Career goal: to build a career at the intersection of finance, technology and social impact, and to start ventures that make financial opportunity more accessible.
